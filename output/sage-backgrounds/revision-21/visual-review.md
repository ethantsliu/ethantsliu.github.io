# Independent visual review

The previous version read as three stacked stamps with similar widths, clean horizontal gaps, and tidy silhouettes. The main artwork felt more atmospheric through overlap, partial occlusion, and forms continuing outside the frame.

The new broad cloud field materially improved this through overlapping masses, recessed shadows, and irregular vapor. In the first homepage capture, however, the wide source was clipped into a nearly straight vertical boundary; the secondary-page capture preserved more of its outer edge.

A separately generated narrow homepage composition corrected that framing. Review of the final desktop capture found distinct protrusions, recesses, a loose lower edge, and naturally varying white space beside the text, with no further material framing issue identified. This is a visual judgment, not a substitute for the user's preference.
