Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ASCII illustration for the small upper-left margin of a minimalist website.
Reference image: the supplied cloud artwork is the ONLY style and palette reference. Create a new subject, matching its visible typewriter-character construction and luminous ivory / muted blue-gray shadows. Do not depict clouds in the new image.
NONNEGOTIABLE MEDIUM: the entire subject must be constructed ONLY from clearly recognizable, upright, monospaced ASCII characters: @ # % O o * + = - : . / |. Individual letters and punctuation, with holes and clear spaces between them, must be visible. No painting or photograph beneath the characters. No engraving, stippling, hatching, grain, continuous filled areas, vector outlines, smooth gradients or realistic stone/feathers. Build shape, light and shadow from colored glyphs and their density, like the reference.
Important display scale: this illustration will be only 140–180 CSS pixels wide on the page. Use a COARSE ASCII grid, at most 24–28 rows of large characters and about 28–36 columns across the whole canvas, with characters roughly 45–60 native pixels tall on a roughly 1200px canvas. These must be substantially larger glyphs than the reference, so they remain recognizably textual when reduced. Avoid fine microtype that collapses into dots. All characters stay upright on horizontal baselines.
Palette: sample the reference's luminous ivory and cream highlights, warm pale ochre, muted cool blue-gray shadow characters. Gentle but clearly visible character contrast. No black silhouette, saturated green, yellow or orange. The existing website will add the same golden-hour filter used on the clouds.
Composition: one complete isolated subject with roughly 8% clear margin on all sides, no clipping. Pure white #ffffff background and all negative space, opaque, perfectly flat, no off-white rectangle, paper texture, gradient or cast shadow. No text words, captions, logo or watermark.

Subject: one complete elegant crescent moon made entirely of characters, its illuminated left and lower curve thick enough to hold several rows of glyphs. The open right interior is entirely blank white paper. Warm ivory and muted blue-gray characters model the curve gently; glyphs become sparse and pale near the tips. No underlying solid crescent, no outer drawn arc, no twinkling icons. The crescent should be luminous and tactile in the same way as the reference clouds. Square canvas. Render on white for the website's existing night filter to convert it to a golden moon on black.
