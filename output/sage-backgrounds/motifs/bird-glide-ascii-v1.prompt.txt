Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one isolated ASCII bird illustration, to be placed in the open sky beside an existing cloud on a minimalist personal website.
Reference: the supplied cloud is solely the palette and ASCII rendering reference. Generate ONLY a new bird, no clouds in this asset. The website will compose the bird with the exact existing cloud artwork.
Medium: ALL visible bird forms are built from large upright MONOSPACED ASCII characters @ # % O o + = : . / *. The glyphs themselves create volume and edges, with clean white gaps and open counters. No painting underneath, no realistic feather texture, line drawing, engraving, continuous silhouette or vector shape. Glyphs stay upright on horizontal baselines regardless of the bird's direction.
Display scale: this entire asset will be only about 120 pixels wide on the page. Use roughly 100–200 large bold readable characters in total, about 20–26 columns across the wingspan and 10–16 rows high. Each character should be around 45–65 native pixels tall on a roughly 1200px-wide canvas. Coarse but graceful ASCII, not dense tiny glyphs that collapse into dots.
Palette: luminous pale ivory and cream, muted cool blue-gray shadow characters, a few pale warm gold characters; matching the reference cloud. Favor moderately dark blue-gray glyphs at the body and lower wing edges for definition, lighter sparse characters at upper edges. No black silhouette, saturated green or orange.
Framing: landscape 3:2 canvas. Single bird only, complete silhouette and all wing tips within generous 10% pure-white margins. The canvas and all empty space are perfectly flat opaque pure white #ffffff. No background tint, paper texture, cast shadow, haze, gradients, decorations, words, watermark or frame.
Avoid the previous pose: NO one giant upright spear-shaped wing, NO long forked streaming tail, NO huge rounded breast, NO rigid V or M logo silhouette. The bird must feel as if it belongs in open air among clouds.

Pose: a small distant bird gliding calmly, seen from slightly below and to one side, heading to the right. Two broad gently arched wings extended almost level, gently bent at the wrists. The nearer wing sweeps down-left and the farther wing bends softly back-right, creating a natural shallow asymmetric curve rather than a straight bar. Compact body, small unobtrusive head, short fan-shaped tail. Emphasize a convincing floating gliding posture, a low wide shape with subtle organic contour, not a perched bird or dramatic upward flap.
